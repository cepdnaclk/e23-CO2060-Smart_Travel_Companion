CO2060 • SOFTWARE SYSTEMS DESIGN PROJECT

Project Overview

Smart Travel Companion brings important travel-planning activities together in one platform, helping users move from destination discovery to an organized travel plan.

01

The Problem

Trip planning can be fragmented across maps, notes, bookings, recommendations, and different travel information sources.

02

Who It Serves

Local explorers and foreign tourists who need a convenient way to organize travel plans before and during a trip.

03

The Solution

One travel companion for discovering places, planning trips, generating itineraries, navigating locations, and managing trip details.
